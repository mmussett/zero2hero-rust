# Zero to Hero: Rust

A self-paced, project-based 30-day curriculum — from absolute beginner to production-minded Rust developer.

---

## Curriculum

| Week | Days | Theme |
|------|------|-------|
| [Week 1 — Foundations](#week-1--foundations) | 1–7 | Toolchain, ownership, structs, enums, modules |
| [Week 2 — Core Language](#week-2--core-language) | 8–14 | Collections, traits, generics, closures, testing |
| [Week 3 — Intermediate Patterns](#week-3--intermediate-patterns) | 15–21 | Trait objects, smart pointers, async, I/O, WASM |
| [Week 4 — Production](#week-4--production) | 22–30 | HTTP, databases, errors, logging, macros, publishing |
